See hunspell-COPYING.txt for the upstream notice and MPL-1.1.txt for the MPL
license option. The MIT wrapper notice is in ../hunspell-wasm/LICENSE.
The corresponding engine source and wrapper/build scripts for the shipped
WebAssembly binary are included in this directory as hunspell-source.tar.gz
and hunspell-wrapper-source.tar.gz. Extract the engine at native/vendor/hunspell
inside the wrapper checkout to reproduce its documented build. The build uses
Emscripten 6.0.6. Its third-party notices are in emscripten-LICENSE.txt.

collapse-white-space 2.1.0. Their MIT notices are provided alongside this file.
